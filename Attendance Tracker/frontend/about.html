About Attendance Tracker

Your digital solution for managing attendance efficiently

Overview

Attendance Tracker is a modern web-based system designed to simplify attendance management for educational institutions. Our system provides real-time tracking, detailed reports, and valuable insights to help students and educators stay informed about attendance patterns.

How It Works

1. Mark Attendance

Teachers record attendance digitally during class sessions

2. Real-time Sync

Data is instantly updated to the cloud-based system

3. Track & Analyze

Students and teachers can view attendance statistics and trends

Access your attendance data from any device
